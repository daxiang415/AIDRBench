No candidate is drawn as zero physical capacity when none was selected. A selected request that fails confirmation stays visible as a failed request. A post-hoc successful full-request comparator is not promoted to the selected offer. Zero missed work uses a 1e-7 GPU-h numerical tolerance and is not a measured application-quality guarantee.

Independent model scenarios are n=300 in structural confirmation. Production timing has eight observed weeks with ten synthetic realizations per week; plot week-level outcomes and label aggregate counts descriptively, without treating 80 realizations as 80 independent weeks. Preserve all tested fractions, conditions and failed runs in Source Data even when the main figure concentrates on the prespecified reference comparison.

Offer capacities are selected grid values, not estimates with power-unit confidence intervals. Probability bounds accompany success fractions in the designated panel/caption or SI table. Accounting curves are deterministic functions of declared prices and empirical complete-series cost draws; do not add sampling error bars as decoration. Total-quantile contributions use shared rank-interpolation weights, not summed marginal quantiles.

Quantitative grid, about 183 mm wide. Main figures target 150–180 mm height; supporting figure may use a taller page. Keep every glyph at least 5 pt, editable SVG/PDF text, PNG 300 dpi and TIFF 600 dpi. Check all panels for collision, uncropped labels, truthful axes and comparable uncertainty. Copy the other seven figures without changing their evidence. Final panel allocation may be reduced for clarity after the confirmed evidence is available, with the change recorded.
