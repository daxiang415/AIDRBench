## Supplementary Figure 2 | Four-GPU power calibration

![Supplementary Figure 2: four-GPU power measurements and held-out check](figures/nature_supplementary_v1/supplementary_figure_2.png)

Per-GPU points within a run remain repeated observations; independent four-GPU run means define the uncertainty unit. Calibration repeats 1–2 are separated from held-out repeat 3. Board-power measurements do not directly measure node overhead or facility cooling.

## Supplementary Figure 3 | Observation and information timing

The fixed 63-dimensional `firm_v5` interface exposes bounded current and forecast information. Requests appear only after declared notice; future workload arrivals remain hidden. Compute debt and complete outcomes are audit state rather than privileged future inputs.

## Supplementary Figure 4 | Representative frozen transition

![Supplementary Figure 4: representative validation trajectory](figures/nature_supplementary_v1/supplementary_figure_4.png)

The representative episode is selected by the deterministic minimum-validation-seed rule, not by its outcome. It illustrates arrivals, execution, aggregate action, PCC delivery, backlog and compute debt for the frozen robust-MPC specification. Certification relies on the full independent ensemble, not this trajectory.
